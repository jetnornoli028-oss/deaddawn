Dead Dawn - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026. Dead Dawn ("the game") is published by Dead games. Contact: jetnornoli028@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account data (only if you create an account): your chosen username (stored as a technical sign-in address ending in @deaddawn.game) and your password. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication and are stored only in hashed form; we cannot see them.
  • Game progress: your story save, trophies, settings, best endless wave and tutorial status, stored under your account so you can restore it on another device.
  • Leaderboard entries: the display name you use and your score, wave, kills, time and date. These are public in the leaderboard.
  • Trophy backup: a randomly generated recovery key and your trophy data, stored so you can restore trophies.

We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos or advertising identifiers. The game contains no ads and no third-party analytics.

How it is used

Only to run the game: signing you in, saving and restoring progress, and showing leaderboards.

Where it is stored

On your device, and for accounts, backups and leaderboards, on Google Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database), which processes data on our behalf. See Firebase privacy information. Data is sent over HTTPS.

Sharing

We do not sell your data or share it with anyone other than Google Firebase as our service provider. Leaderboard display names and scores are visible to other players.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account and all saved progress at any time in the game (Menu → Account → Delete Account), or on the web at https://jetnornoli028-oss.github.io/deaddawn/delete-account.html. You can also email us to request deletion. Deleting your account removes your authentication record, cloud save and trophy backup. Past leaderboard entries consist only of a display name and score and are not linked to your account by a unique ID; they may remain on the leaderboard.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Contact us if you believe a child has created an account and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above.
